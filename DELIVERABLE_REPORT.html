🎯 Implemented Features (Zero Artificial Gamification)

⚔️ Minigame: Pawn Wars

8 vs 8 pawns match focused on pawn movement, diagonal captures, promotion, and blocking. First pawn to reach opposite rank wins. Includes undo and hints.

🦄 Minigame: The Hungry Knight

L-jump mastery in 3 progressive stages. The child must collect all golden stars ⭐ while dodging fiery lava traps 🔥 with instant sound and haptic alerts.

🤖 Expressive Reactive Sparky

Mascot with 4 vector micro-expressions: Normal (cheerful smile), Thinking (concentrated squint & spark), Surprised (open mouth on captures/checks), and Celebrating (party eyes & confetti).

🎨 Magic Realm Board Themes

Modal palette selector: Magic Fantasy (Lavender/Cream), Emerald Forest (Green/Mint), Ice Kingdom (Arctic Blue), and Candy Land (Pink/Vanilla). Persisted locally.

📳 Gentle Haptic Feedback (18ms)

Subtle tactile pulse with Android VibrationEffect on moves, captures, and stars. Respects mute mode completely for quiet environments.

🐣 Adaptive Difficulty for Sparky

1-tap selector pills: Toddler (never captures Queen, high forgiveness), Friend (instructional balance), and Champion (tactical play defending pieces and seeking checks).
