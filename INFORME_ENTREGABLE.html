🛠️ Comprehensive Bug Audit & Kids UX Improvements (v1.1)

Conducted extensive research on child-friendly chess apps (ChessKid, Lichess Learn, Magnus Kingdom) and resolved edge cases:

  • 1. Tutorial Turn Freeze Resolution: In tutorial drills, making a legal non-target move previously flipped the turn to BLACK, permanently locking the child's white piece. Fix: The turn is locked to the player's color (WHITE), friendly feedback is displayed ("Almost! Aim for the star ⭐ or tap Hint 💡"), a soft audio cue plays, and the piece automatically glides back to start after 850 ms without requiring a level restart.
  • 2. On-Screen Hint Button (Hint 💡): Added an always-visible button in challenge drills that computes the vector to the target star and paints a bright golden guide arrow with encouragement from Sparky.
  • 3. Instant Retry Button (Retry 🔄): Dedicated 1-tap restart button for immediate level resets at any moment.
  • 4. Level 7 Blocked Queen Ray Fix: Discovered that the White King at (2, 4) obstructed the vertical line of the Queen at (6, 4) to (1, 4). Shifted the King to (2, 3), ensuring full protection of the mate square while clearing the path for the championship checkmate.
  • 5. Practice Game Race Condition Shield: Added isComputerThinking state and callback cancellations to prevent board state desynchronization if the child taps Undo or Restart while the AI is calculating its move. Disabled interaction once checkmate/stalemate is reached.
  • 6. Defensive Audio Engine: Caught Throwable in SoundEffects.kt to ensure 100% crash immunity across diverse Android audio drivers.
